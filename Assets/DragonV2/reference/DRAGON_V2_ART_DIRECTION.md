# DragonV2 Art Direction

DragonV2 is a clean visual pipeline and must not copy failed DragonV1 art or
animation decisions.

## Visual Target

- cute small desktop companion dragon
- soft anime / watercolor / chibi style
- white and pale icy-blue palette may be kept, but do not copy failed DragonV1 exactly
- large expressive eyes
- small rounded body
- compact readable silhouette
- calm enough for long-term desktop companion use
- compatible beside CharacterV2 without stealing visual focus

## Wing Requirements

- exactly one pair of wings
- natural organic dragon wings, not geometric panels
- proportionally smaller and softer than the failed DragonV1 versions
- clear shoulder/back attachment
- soft curved bones/fingers
- coherent membrane from root to trailing edge
- no extra hidden mini wings
- no crystal-panel wings
- no wireframe wings
- no fan-shaped artificial panels
- no disconnected decorative wing objects

## Animation Constraints For Future Stages

- no body identity drift between frames
- do not use full-frame AI regeneration for every animation frame unless manually approved
- body, wings, tail, and optional shadow must be separated before rigging
- wing rig must rotate/fold around fixed shoulder/back hinges
- animation starts only after static design and layer split are human-accepted

## DragonV1 Lessons To Preserve

- oversized wings caused desktop discomfort and scale mismatch
- panel/fan-like membranes read as fake props, not dragon anatomy
- leftover mini-wing artifacts created a two-wing-system illusion
- unstable hinge placement broke the creature's body language
- repeated repair of the failed base made the design worse
