# Dragon Visual Spec

Status: PROVISIONAL, usable as concept reference.

## Identity Anchors

- Character type: tiny soft book-page magic dragon familiar.
- Role: playful shoulder pet and study companion familiar.
- Mood: soft, curious, slightly clumsy, non-threatening.
- Body proportion: large rounded head, compact body, short limbs, short curled tail.

## Body Structure

- Head: rounded, wide, cute mascot face.
- Body: small pear-shaped body with soft belly.
- Legs: short and rounded, with tiny blue claws.
- Tail: short curled scroll/paper tail with rolled page tip.
- Wings: two page-like wings with pale blue membranes and page-line markings.
- Horns: two curled page-scroll horns or soft page tabs.

## Book And Data Elements

- Forehead mark: open-book symbol in pale blue.
- Wing surfaces: book-page line texture.
- Tail tip: curled scroll/page form.
- Data dots: cold white and pale cyan points around wings/tail.
- Pale gold letters: optional, used sparingly near the dragon.

## Default Expression

- Big blue eyes.
- Tiny smiling mouth.
- Soft cheek blush.
- Innocent and slightly mischievous, never aggressive.

## Relationship To Main Character

- Default scale: about 35%-45% of the main character's head height when perched.
- It should fit on her shoulder, beside a book, or in a small desktop nest.
- It must not become the visual center when shown with the main character.

## Allowed Simplifications

- Reduce page-line density on wings.
- Reduce glow dots.
- Simplify horn scroll lines.
- Simplify belly/page markings.

## Forbidden Changes

- Do not turn it into a realistic reptile.
- Do not add sharp fangs, spines, armor, or aggressive claws.
- Do not replace book-page wings with standard bat wings only.
- Do not remove the scroll tail.
- Do not make it larger than shoulder-pet scale in paired references.
